# Mission Reflection

## Reflection

Among the three cloud platforms, Google Cloud Platform (GCP) impressed me the most. I found its services for Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes interesting. I also learned that GCP can be useful for companies that work with large amounts of data and AI projects. Before this activity, I only knew a little about GCP, but this mission helped me understand it better.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide cloud services such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, and identity management. They also allow companies to use computing resources without having to maintain all the physical hardware themselves. However, each platform has different services and strengths, so users need to choose the one that fits their needs.

Business requirements are important when choosing a cloud platform. A company should consider its budget, type of application, number of users, security needs, existing technologies, and future growth. For example, Azure can be a good choice for a company that already uses many Microsoft products, while GCP can be a good choice for a company focused on AI and Machine Learning.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider is not just about picking the most popular platform. I need to understand what the client needs first and then compare the available services. This activity helped me practice making decisions based on actual business requirements.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved after completing this mission. I added more organized documentation about AWS, Azure, and GCP and learned how to compare their services. I also became more comfortable using Markdown and organizing my work in GitHub. Overall, this mission helped me improve my understanding of cloud computing and gave me more confidence in working with cloud technologies.
